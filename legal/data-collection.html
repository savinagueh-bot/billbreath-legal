This document describes what BillBreath collects, why, how long we keep it, and with whom it may be shared. It complements the Privacy Policy. If there is a conflict, the Privacy Policy and applicable law control.

1. Scope

Applies to the BillBreath mobile app (Expo / React Native) as operated by Iowa Structured Cabling LLC in the United States.

3. Legal bases / reasons we process (plain English)

U.S. apps often describe purposes rather than GDPR “legal bases.” We process data because:

  • You asked us to provide the service (contract / service delivery)
  • You consented (notifications; optional account)
  • Legitimate interests in securing the App and preventing abuse, balanced against your rights
  • Legal compliance when required

4. Collection methods

  • Directly from you — forms in the App; support email
  • Automatically — auth session handled by Supabase SDK; local storage writes for bills/settings; OS notification APIs
  • From stores / billing partners — when real IAP is enabled, purchase validation via Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat

We do not scrape your email inbox or SMS for bills in this version.

5. Use of each category (why)

  • Account data — sign-in, password reset flows (via Supabase), account support
  • Bill & paycheck inputs — calculate “left this paycheck,” list bills, mark paid, schedule reminders
  • Reminder prefs — decide when local notifications fire
  • Premium status — unlock insights and longer reminder lead times
  • Support email — answer questions, investigate bugs, process deletion requests

6. Retention

Data Typical retention
On-device bills, paycheck, reminder prefs Until you delete entries, clear app storage, or uninstall
Guest session Until sign-out/clear/uninstall
Supabase auth account Until deletion request or account closure
Local Premium demo entitlement Until cleared in-app or app data reset
Store subscription records Per Apple/Google (and RevenueCat) retention; App may cache entitlement status while installed
Support emails As needed for the request, then routine business email retention

We do not promise indefinite cloud backups of bills in the MVP because bills are primarily on-device.

7. Sharing and processors

Recipient Role Data involved

RevenueCat (if enabled) Subscription status helper Purchase tokens / subscriber ids
Iowa Structured Cabling LLC Operator Support emails; limited operational access
Law enforcement / regulators Legal process Only if legally required

No sale of personal information for monetary consideration is intended. No bank aggregators in this version.

8. Cross-border transfer

Auth and infrastructure providers may process data in the United States or other regions they operate. By using a cloud account, you understand your account data may leave your local device.

9. Security measures (high level)

  • Passwords handled by Supabase Auth (hashed/managed by provider)
  • Local data protected by device lock / OS app sandbox
  • HTTPS for network calls to Supabase
  • Least-privilege access for support

Report suspected security issues to support@billbreath.app.

10. Your controls

  • Delete individual bills in the App
  • Sign out; clear guest session
  • Disable notifications
  • Cancel Premium in store settings
  • Request account deletion via support@billbreath.app
  • Uninstall the App

11. Changes to collection practices

If we add bank linking, cloud bill sync, analytics, or ads, we will update this Policy, the Privacy Policy, and store “Data safety” / nutrition labels before those features collect new data types.
