This Privacy Policy explains how BillBreath (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use the BillBreath mobile app. Plain language first; details below.

1. Quick summary

Topic Current practice

Account Optional email/password via Supabase Auth; or guest (on-device)

2. Who is responsible

Iowa Structured Cabling LLC operates BillBreath for users in the United States. Contact privacy or support requests at support@billbreath.app (placeholder — replace before publish).

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Account: email address, password (handled by Supabase; we do not store your password in the App’s local bill database), and optional display name
  • Guest mode: a local guest session identifier on your device (not a cloud account)
  • Bills & money planning you enter: bill names, amounts, due dates, paid/unpaid status, optional notes; paycheck amount, next payday, pay frequency
  • Support: messages you send to support@billbreath.app

3.2 Information collected automatically (limited)

  • Device / app technical data that the operating system or Expo runtime may process for the App to run (for example, crash diagnostics if you later enable a crash reporter — disclose any such SDK before shipping)
  • Notification permission status if you enable reminders
  • Subscription / purchase status on device for Premium; when live store billing is enabled, Apple, Google, and/or RevenueCat will process purchase tokens as described in their policies

We do not currently require third-party advertising or marketing analytics SDKs for the core MVP. If that changes, we will update this Policy and store disclosures before enabling them.

3.3 What we do not collect in this version

  • Bank login credentials or live bank account balances via aggregators (no Plaid or similar)
  • Automatic transaction feeds
  • Government ID numbers, SSN, or credit file data for credit reporting

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account (Supabase Auth)
  • Provide bill lists, leftover calculations, paycheck settings, and optional local reminders
  • Unlock and manage Premium features when you subscribe
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms

We do not use your bill list to sell you third-party financial products in the current MVP description.

5. How information is stored

Data Where (MVP)
Bills, paycheck settings, reminder prefs, local Premium demo entitlement On your device (e.g., AsyncStorage)
Auth session for signed-in users Managed by Supabase with session persistence on device
Guest session On your device only
Real App Store / Play purchases (when enabled) Processed by Apple/Google (and RevenueCat if used); receipt/entitlement status may sync to the App

Cloud sync of bills is not the default MVP behavior. If we add server-side bill storage later, we will update this Policy and notify users as required.

6. Sharing

We share information only as needed:

  • Service providers: Supabase (authentication); Apple / Google (app distribution and in-app purchases); RevenueCat if enabled for subscription management
  • Legal: if required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • Business transfers: if the Company is involved in a merger, sale, or asset transfer, information may transfer under continued confidentiality commitments

We do not sell your personal information as that term is commonly used in consumer privacy laws, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in the current MVP.

7. Notifications

Local reminders are scheduled on your device for unpaid bills you choose to track. You can disable reminders in the App or in system settings. Notification content may include bill names and amounts you entered.

8. Retention

  • Local bill/paycheck data: remains on your device until you delete it in the App, clear app data, or uninstall
  • Guest session: until you sign out/clear data or uninstall
  • Supabase account: retained until you request deletion or we delete inactive accounts as allowed by law
  • Support emails: retained as needed to resolve your request and for legitimate business records

To request account deletion, email support@billbreath.app from the address associated with your account. Device-local data may also need to be cleared by you on the device.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a small consumer app (including reliance on Supabase and platform OS protections). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You can help by using a strong password and keeping your device updated.

10. Children

BillBreath is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information, contact support@billbreath.app and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location (including certain U.S. state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to appeal a denial. Email support@billbreath.app. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

You can:

  • Use guest mode instead of creating an account
  • Decline notification permission
  • Cancel Premium through Apple or Google subscription settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove most on-device data

12. International users

The App is operated from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information may be processed in the U.S. and other countries where our providers operate (including Supabase infrastructure regions they use).

13. Changes

We may update this Policy. We will change the effective date and, where required, provide additional notice. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
