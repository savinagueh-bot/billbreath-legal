BillBreath Refund Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for BillBreath Premium. Free features of BillBreath do not involve payment.

1. How Premium is sold

When Premium is offered through the Apple App Store or Google Play:

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account
  • Renewal, cancellation, and most refunds are controlled by Apple or Google, not by a separate BillBreath checkout page
  • Current advertised prices in the App include approximately $4.99/month and $39.99/year, and may include a free trial (for example, 7 days) as shown at the time of purchase

Development note: Demo / local “purchases” inside early builds do not create real charges and are not eligible for store refunds because no store payment occurred.

2. Canceling a subscription

Cancel anytime to stop future renewals. You generally keep Premium until the end of the current paid (or trial) period, subject to Apple/Google rules.

Apple (iOS)

  1. Open iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions
  2. Select BillBreath
  3. Cancel subscription

Or manage via Apple’s subscriptions help.

Google Play (Android)

  1. Open the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  2. Select BillBreath
  3. Cancel

Or see Google Play subscriptions help.

Uninstalling the App alone does not always cancel a subscription—use the steps above.

3. Free trials

If a free trial is offered:

  • You may be charged when the trial ends unless you cancel first under Apple/Google rules
  • Trial eligibility is determined by Apple/Google (for example, one trial per Apple ID / Google account policies)

4. Refunds

Apple App Store

Refund requests for Apple in-app purchases and subscriptions are typically submitted to Apple, not to Iowa Structured Cabling LLC directly. Use reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple Support. Apple decides refund eligibility under its policies.

Google Play

Refund requests for Play subscriptions and one-time purchases follow Google Play’s refund policies. You may request a refund in Play purchase history where available, or contact Google Play Support. Google decides eligibility under its policies.

Contacting us

If you have trouble canceling, believe you were charged in error, or need help finding the right store tools, email support@billbreath.app. We can try to help you navigate Apple/Google processes. We cannot override Apple or Google refund decisions, and we may not receive funds for store-billed subscriptions in a way that allows us to refund you ourselves.

5. What we generally cannot refund

Examples (subject to store rules and law):

  • Unused time after you cancel mid-period (access usually continues until period end)
  • Failure to cancel before renewal or trial conversion
  • Charges after you forgot you subscribed
  • Dissatisfaction alone where the platform does not grant a refund

If a law in your state requires a different remedy, that law controls.

6. Price changes

Subscription prices may change. Apple/Google typically notify you and require consent where required before a new price renews.
